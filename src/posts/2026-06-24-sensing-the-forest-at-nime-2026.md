[New Interfaces for Musical Expression (NIME)](https://nime.org/) is an international conference about new musical interfaces, their artistic use and the technologies involved in building them.

[NIME 2026](https://nime2026.org) was held from June 23rd to 26th in London, UK, hosted by the [Augmented Instruments Laboratory](https://instrumentslab.org/) at Imperial College London and the [CHAOS Lab](https://thechaoslab.org/) at Loughborough University London (LUL).

[The Centre for Digital Music (C4DM) had a strong presence at the conference](https://c4dm.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/news/2026-06-30.C4DM-at_NIME_2026/), both in terms of numbers and overall impact, including many contributions by C4DM alumni.

I had the privilege to present Sensing the Forest as a performance for the NIME community.
